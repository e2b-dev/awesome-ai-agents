# WaliGPT Addition for awesome-ai-agents

Proposed addition to the **Deployment & Hosting** section of the README:

## Entry

- **[WaliGPT](https://waligpt.com)** - Managed deployment platform for [OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw) agents. Ships with a library of pre-configured agent templates across crypto trading, Polymarket prediction markets, Telegram alpha monitoring, Twitter automation, NFT tracking, Discord moderation, and research — deployable to isolated cloud instances in one click with no infrastructure setup.
